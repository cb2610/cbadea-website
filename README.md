# Cosmin Badea — Personal Academic Website

This repository contains the source code for my personal academic and professional website.

The site presents my work in AI ethics, artificial moral agency, decision-making, interpretability, machine consciousness, and the philosophy of artificial intelligence. It is intended as a public-facing home for my research, teaching, writing, projects, and professional profile.

## About

I am Cosmin Badea, an AI researcher and lecturer working at the intersection of contemporary philosophy, artificial intelligence, moral reasoning, and decision-making.

My work focuses especially on:

- Artificial moral agents
- Moral reasoning in AI
- Decision-making and meta-decision-making
- Interpretability and explainability
- AI ethics in healthcare
- Philosophy of AI and machine consciousness

The website is designed to make this work more accessible to researchers, students, collaborators, and anyone interested in the ethical and philosophical foundations of AI.

## Featured Research Themes

The website gives particular emphasis to several connected research strands:

### The Interpretation Problem

Work on the limits of rule-based moral AI, inspired partly by Wittgensteinian considerations about rule-following, interpretation, and the gap between formal instructions and morally appropriate action.

### MARS: The Multi-valued Action Reasoning System

A formal framework for modelling value-based decision-making in artificial agents, including moral dilemmas, competing values, and structured ethical reasoning.

### Meta-Decision-Making for AI

A research direction focused on how artificial systems can decide not only what to do, but how to decide: which representations, reasons, values, and reasoning procedures are relevant in a given context.

### Applied AI Ethics

Work on healthcare AI, antimicrobial use, breaking bad news, clinician exemplars, interpretability, and responsible AI design.
